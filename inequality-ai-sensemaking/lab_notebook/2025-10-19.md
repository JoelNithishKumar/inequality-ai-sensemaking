## Decisions I made (and why)
- Decision: Use a ratio question ($100 framing) instead of raw dollar amounts.
  Why: Easier for participants and produces a clean numeric accuracy score.
- Decision: Focus on one inequality domain for v1 (wealth, White vs Black).
  Why: Keeps stimuli and scoring simple for the first iteration.
- Decision: Keep stimuli comparable (length, tone, reading level).
  Why: Reduces confounds so message type is the main difference.
