# Benchmark (v1)

## Chosen inequality metric
Median household wealth (U.S.): White vs Black households.

## Participant question format
“If a typical White household has $100 in wealth, about how many dollars does a typical Black household have?”

## Benchmark value (v1)
Using 2022 Survey of Consumer Finances values (as reported in a secondary source):
- White median wealth ≈ $285,000
- Black median wealth ≈ $44,890

Benchmark ratio:
(44,890 / 285,000) * 100 ≈ 15.8

For scoring, we will use:
- benchmark_ratio = 15.8
- (optional) rounded benchmark shown to participants as ~16 for readability
